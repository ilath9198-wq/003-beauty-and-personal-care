Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful guidelines to ensure your salon visit is seamless and relaxing.

Is valet parking available at your Indiranagar studio?
Yes, complimentary white-glove valet parking is provided directly in front of our main building on 100ft Road for all studio clients.
How early should I arrive for my appointment?
We recommend arriving 10 minutes prior to your scheduled time to enjoy our herbal welcome beverage and complete a brief digital skin/scalp diagnostic questionnaire.
What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
We kindly request at least 4 hours notice for standard salon services and 48 hours for bridal makeup trials so our artists can accommodate waitlisted clients.
Are bridal makeup services available on location at hotels/venues?
Yes, our creative director and bridal team travel across Bengaluru, India, and international destination weddings with full mobile studio lighting kits.
