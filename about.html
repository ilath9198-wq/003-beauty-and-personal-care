Our Heritage & Beauty Philosophy

Founded on the premise that true beauty is born where clinical skincare precision meets Parisian artistry and soulful Indian hospitality.

The ÉLAN Story

Elevating the Ritual of Personal Transformation

Established in 2021 by creative director Céline Rao following her decade of runway makeup direction across London Fashion Week and Paris ateliers, ÉLAN was conceived as an antidote to hurried, factory-like salon visits.

Spread across 4,200 sq.ft of serene, warm-toned architecture in Indiranagar, we engineered an environment where appointments are never double-booked, scalp and skin diagnostic consultations precede every treatment, and every formulation touching your skin is dermatologist-certified.

A Tour of Our Treatment Sanctuaries

Every zone in our Indiranagar flagship has been customized for discretion, ergonomic comfort, and clinical precision.

The Hair Artistry Lounge

Equipped with motorized Italian Belmont hydraulic leather chairs, shadowless 5000K daylight LED mirrors, and a separate low-lit wash bar with shiatsu massage recliners.

The Clinical Dermal Sanctuary

Individual sound-proof suites fitted with medical-grade Hydrafacial MD vortex machines, oxygen peel delivery systems, and organic botanical hot towel steamers.

The Imperial Bridal Suite

A private 400 sq.ft suite for brides and their families featuring a 360-degree trial stage, champagne bar, private en-suite powder room, and dedicated jewelry styling desks.

Custom ergonomic tables with built-in dust vacuum filtration, German Saeyang e-files, and an archive of over 300 non-toxic vegan Japanese gel colors.
